How Points Work

TOP 10 SCORE

Only the top ten finishers earn points on a Grand Prix Sunday. Win races, finish consistently, and the points add up across the season.

Tyres

GRIP VS DURABILITY

Softer tyres are faster but wear out sooner; harder tyres last longer but are slower. In a dry race every driver must use at least two different slick compounds — which forces a pit stop and creates strategy.

Title #1

Drivers' Championship

Every driver's points across all races are added up. The one with the most at season's end is the World Champion. It rewards individual consistency, race pace and racecraft.

Title #2

Constructors' Championship

Each team's two drivers combine their points. This crowns the best team — car, engineers, strategy and pit crew — and determines how prize money is shared out.
